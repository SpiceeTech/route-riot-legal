Route Riot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Route Riot ("we", "us") is a mobile game operated by SPICEETECH (PTY) LTD ("SpiceeTech"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and what choices you have.

The short version

Route Riot has no accounts and no sign-in, and we don't run any servers for it. Your progress is saved only on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some data from your device to show and measure those ads.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (coins, your depot, upgrades and settings such as music volume) is saved in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and we can't see it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Route Riot is free and uses ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google. Ads in Route Riot are always your choice: you watch one only when you tap a button to get something in return, such as a bonus round or skipping a break.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising ID.
  • Approximate location: estimated from your IP address (not your precise GPS location).
  • App interactions: for example, which ads you were shown and whether you tapped them.
  • Diagnostics: technical details such as crash logs and performance data of the ads SDK.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is covered by Google's own policies: see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Who we share data with

We don't collect your personal data ourselves, and we don't sell any data. The only data that leaves your phone is what the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google, as described above.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is only on your phone, you are in full control of it: uninstalling Route Riot, or clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Route Riot → Storage → Clear data), permanently deletes it. We hold no copy, so we can't restore it either. For data held by Google for advertising, reset or delete your advertising ID as described above, or see your choices at My Ad Center.

Children's privacy

Route Riot is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, for example if the game adds online features, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, let players know in the game.
